# Brand onboarding vertical slice critique

## Verdict

Ship for the requested vertical slice. The Signal Dossier direction is cohesive, responsive, and visually specific; a fresh finish review found no in-scope blockers.

## Assessment A — human review

Nielsen score: 21/40 (acceptable, with follow-on UX work). The strongest qualities are the authored dossier metaphor, focused sequential onboarding, clear real-data reward state, and solid foundations for labels, focus, reduced motion, and async status.

Priority follow-on findings:

1. The current slice explains AI-derived positioning more strongly than naano's creator-marketplace and qualified-click differentiation. Carry that connection into the creator-discovery slice.
2. Generated profile data is saved after one pass without edit, evidence review, or regenerate controls. Treat this as a draft-and-review workflow when profile management is built.
3. The empty dashboard intentionally has no live creator-discovery action. Replace its disabled navigation with real destinations as those slices ship.

## Assessment B — detector and browser evidence

- Static detector on `src/app`: no findings.
- Browser detector on the unauthenticated auth route: 15 undersized functional labels, two false-positive nested-card hits caused by clipped overlapping tabs, and one intentional display-heading tight-leading hit.
- The functional labels were raised to 0.7rem (11.2px). The false positives were not changed because they are part of the chosen visual grammar and do not represent nested cards or body-copy leading.
- Protected routes were also inspected separately through a complete authenticated browser flow and responsive screenshots.

## Resolution

- Fixed undersized progress, tab, evidence, workspace, pain, and product-fit labels.
- Simplified auth mode controls to honest pressed-button semantics.
- Removed an inert dashboard CTA and implementation-language copy.
- Corrected copy that implied generated profiles were already editable.
- Deliberately deferred profile review/edit/regenerate controls and creator-marketplace continuation because they are separate feature slices, not incomplete wiring in this requested empty-shell scope.

Questions skipped: the two remaining priority questions belong to explicitly deferred feature slices, so no decision is required to ship this one.
